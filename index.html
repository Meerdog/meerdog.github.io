Shower Vibes

A generative ambient synthesizer with four tracks that create evolving soundscapes. Each track has a cat companion that dances when playing!

🐱 THE CATS

  • Drone — Calico (orange, black & white patches)
  • Bell — Tuxedo (black with white chest)
  • Drum 1 — Orange Norwegian Forest Cat (fluffy!)
  • Drum 2 — Bengal (spotted/striped pattern)

🎭 SCENES

Six pre-built harmonious configurations where all tracks complement each other:

  • 🌅 Ambient Dawn — Warm major chords, gentle 72 BPM
  • 🌌 Deep Space — Haunting minor 7ths, slow 60 BPM
  • 🌲 Forest Rain — Natural minor with organic rhythms
  • 💎 Crystal Cave — Suspended chords, high resonant tones
  • 🌊 Ocean Drift — Flowing arps with long reverb tails
  • 🌙 Night Garden — Major 7ths with sidechain ducking

💡 Press the ☀/🌙 button to toggle between day and night modes!

🎹 DRONE (Track A) — Calico Cat

Layered oscillators creating rich pad sounds:

  • Wave — Oscillator shape (Sine=warm, Saw=bright, Tri=mellow, Sqr=hollow)
  • Root — Base frequency in Hz
  • Chord — Harmony type (Major, Minor, 7ths, Suspended, Add9)
  • Voices — Number of layered oscillators (1-8)
  • Detune — Pitch spread between voices for width/chorus effect
  • Drift — Slow filter and pitch modulation for evolving texture
  • Grain — Amplitude shimmer/granular effect

🔔 BELL (Track B) — Tuxedo Cat

Generative melodic arpeggiator with 11 unique algorithms:

  • Random — Pure chance selection
  • Drunk — Small wandering steps (±1 note)
  • Gravity — Tends toward lower notes
  • Bounce — Reflects off scale boundaries
  • Spiral — Expanding intervals from center
  • Breathe — Smooth up/down sweep
  • Cluster — Groups of adjacent notes
  • Fibonacci — Jumps based on Fibonacci sequence
  • Weave — Alternates between low and high regions
  • Echo — Repeating phrases with variation
  • Shimmer — Octave-shifted cascades

Glide adds portamento (smooth pitch slides between notes).

🥁 DRUMS (Tracks 1 & 2) — Forest Cat & Bengal

Euclidean rhythm generators that mathematically distribute beats:

  • Steps — Total pattern length (1-32)
  • Fills — Number of hits spread evenly across steps
  • Rotate — Shifts the pattern start point
  • Division — Time subdivision (quarter, eighth, etc.)
  • Tune — Pitch of the drum sound
  • Decay — How long each hit sustains
  • Swing — Delays off-beats for groove feel

💡 Drum 2 can switch between PERC and BELL modes — BELL mode plays melodic tones instead of percussion!

🎚️ MIXER FADERS

Each track (A, B, 1, 2) has four faders:

  • Volume — Main output level
  • Reverb — Send to reverb effect (space/ambience)
  • Delay — Send to delay effect (echoes)
  • Filter — Cutoff frequency for tone shaping

Click the colored labels above Filter faders to cycle filter types: LP (lowpass), HP (highpass), BP (bandpass).

🎛️ EFFECTS

  • CHORUS — Adds stereo width and shimmer to the effects
  • DUCK — Sidechain compression: drums reduce other tracks for a "pumping" effect

🔄 POLYRHYTHM

Preset ratios that set complementary step counts for both drums:

  • 3:4 — Classic polyrhythm, resolves every 12 beats
  • 4:5 — Slightly more complex, resolves every 20
  • 5:7 — Very complex, resolves every 35
  • 3:5 — Interesting interplay, resolves every 15

🎵 DRUM 2 MODE

  • PERC — Standard percussion sounds
  • BELL — Melodic bell tones (use Tune to set pitch)

🎬 SONG MODE

Automatically creates a 3-minute ambient journey that builds and fades:

Timeline Phases

  • ✨ Intro (0-18s) — Drone fades in from silence
  • 🌅 Awakening (18-45s) — Melodic bells join gradually
  • 🌊 Rising (45-81s) — First rhythm enters and builds
  • 🔥 Crescendo (81-117s) — All tracks peak, chorus activates
  • ⭐ Peak (117-144s) — Maximum intensity plateau
  • 🌙 Fading (144-166s) — Rhythms fade out
  • 💫 Outro (166-180s) — Gentle fade to silence

Scene Selection

Choose which preset to use for Song Mode:

  • Current — Uses your current manual settings
  • Scene names — Loads that scene first
  • 🎲 Random — Picks a random scene

Randomness Slider

Controls variation during playback:

  • 0% — Completely predictable, follows the scene exactly
  • 50% — Moderate variation in patterns and effects
  • 100% — Maximum chaos: patterns, drift, and effects change frequently

💡 During Song Mode, all controls are locked. Press STOP or Escape to regain control.

⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

  • 1, 2, 3, 4 — Toggle tracks on/off
  • Space — Toggle currently selected track
  • A — Play all tracks
  • Escape — Stop all tracks and song mode

Navigation

  • ↑ / ↓ — Move parameter selection
  • ← / → — Adjust selected parameter
  • Shift + ← / → — Switch between tracks

Features

  • S — Start/stop Song Mode
  • R — Randomize all tracks
  • V — Toggle accordion view
  • D — Toggle day/night mode
  • H — Show this help

📐 ACCORDION VIEW

Press ☰ or V to switch to stacked view where tracks expand one at a time. Great for mobile or focused editing!
