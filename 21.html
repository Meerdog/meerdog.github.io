21 Knob: Strategy & Rules

The Goal: Score exactly 21 points, then hit the "Victory Knob" to win.
1. Power Levels (Risk vs. Reward)
  • Power 1 (Lob): +1 to your dice roll, but gives the opponent 3 free hexes of movement.
  • Power 2 (Drive): Normal shot. Opponent gets 1 free hex.
  • Power 3 (Bullet): -2 to your dice roll! But the opponent gets 0 free hexes. Every step costs stamina.
2. Stamina & Penalties
  • Moving costs Stamina. If you move 3+ hexes in a turn, you are Exhausted (-1 to roll).
  • If you have no stamina left, you must Dive (Stamina=0, forced Power 1, -2 penalty).
  • Shooting from the far Left/Right lane imposes an Off-Axis (-1) penalty.
3. The Door Targets
  • Center (TN 2+): Safe. Bottom (TN 4+): Hard drop shot.
  • The Knob (TN 6): Scores 3 Points! If penalties make a 6 impossible, you can "Push the Limit" and pray for a Natural 6.
